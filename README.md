### 🚀 About Me

My name is **Juan** and I'm a software developer based in Brazil, more specifically in Campinas, São Paulo. I'm passionate about technology and about turning real-world problems into working software.

I work primarily with **C# (.NET 8)** and **TypeScript**, using frameworks such as **Angular**, **Node.js** and **Next.js**, with **PostgreSQL** as my main database. At work, I build corporate systems and process automations — including an automation tool with **.NET + Playwright** that turned a full workday of manual tasks into minutes.

Currently, I'm building **[PhysioFlow](COLE_O_LINK_DO_REPO_AQUI)** — a physiotherapy clinic management SaaS — and **JuanFinance**, a personal finance app. I care about **Clean Architecture** and software design best practices.

Beyond coding, I enjoy the gym, playing football and learning guitar. 🎸

🎓 Computer Science student — graduating Dec 2026.
